# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ViennaGeoAI welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Project spaces should remain professional, technically focused, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, data, and evidence
- assume good faith while still asking for reproducible evidence when needed
- welcome useful questions and acknowledge uncertainty
- give credit for contributions and sources
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, and personal attacks
- avoid publishing another person's private information without permission
- keep security-sensitive reports out of public issues

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- insulting, demeaning, discriminatory, or sexually harassing language or imagery
- sustained disruption of technical discussions
- deliberate misrepresentation of another contributor's work
- doxxing or other publication of private information
- credible threats or encouragement of harm
- retaliation against good-faith bug, security, or conduct reports

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project documentation, and other spaces where someone is representing the ViennaGeoAI project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation when behavior is inconsistent with this policy. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and focused on protecting a constructive project environment.

For conduct concerns that should not be public, contact the repository maintainer privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile.

## Attribution

This project code of conduct is intentionally concise and project-specific. It is inspired by common open-source community standards while being maintained as part of ViennaGeoAI.
